# Regular Fund vs Direct Fund - Quick Guide

## Your Situation

You have **2 Regular Plan** mutual funds:
1. **SBI Small Cap Fund Regular Plan** (₹1,88,211)
2. **SBI Focused Fund Regular Plan** (₹2,07,773)

## Common Misconception ❌

**WRONG**: "If I stop the SIP, the expense ratio will reduce"
**RIGHT**: "The plan type (Regular/Direct) is PERMANENT until I actively switch it"

## How Expense Ratio Works

### Regular Plan (What you have)
- **Expense Ratio**: ~1.5% to 2.5% per year
- **Paid to**: Distributor/Agent commission (40-50%) + Fund management
- **Continues**: FOREVER, even after stopping SIP
- **Applied on**: Your ENTIRE investment amount every year

### Direct Plan (What you should have)
- **Expense Ratio**: ~0.5% to 1.5% per year (50-60% lower!)
- **No commission**: All money goes to fund management
- **Better returns**: More money stays invested for you

## Your Current Loss (Estimated)

**SBI Small Cap Regular**: ₹1,88,211 × 1% extra fee = **₹1,882/year wasted**
**SBI Focused Regular**: ₹2,07,773 × 1% extra fee = **₹2,078/year wasted**
**Total waste**: **₹3,960 PER YEAR** (and growing as your corpus grows!)

## What Stopping SIP Does

✅ Stops new investments
✅ Stops new units being added
❌ Does NOT change the plan from Regular to Direct
❌ Does NOT reduce expense ratio
❌ Existing units stay Regular FOREVER (unless you switch)

## How to Actually Reduce Expense Ratio

### Option 1: Switch to Direct Plan (Recommended)
1. Contact SBI Mutual Fund (call customer care or visit branch)
2. Request "Plan Change" from Regular to Direct
3. **Benefits**:
   - ✅ No exit load
   - ✅ No capital gains tax
   - ✅ Same units, just lower expense ratio
   - ✅ Folio number remains same
4. **Limitation**: Not all AMCs allow this (SBI usually does)

### Option 2: Redeem & Reinvest in Direct
1. Redeem Regular plan units
2. Immediately invest in Direct plan
3. **Cons**:
   - ❌ Pay exit load if within 1 year (~1%)
   - ❌ Pay capital gains tax (LTCG 12.5% if >1 year)
   - ❌ New folio number
4. **Only worth it**: If gains are minimal or if held <1 year

### Option 3: Start New SIPs in Direct, Keep Regular
1. Keep existing Regular plan investments as-is
2. Start all NEW investments in Direct plans only
3. **Result**: Future money saves fees, old money still wastes fees
